About Us

Our Story

Our travel agency operates under the umbrella of G.C.S. Holdings (Pvt) Ltd, a diversified Sri Lankan enterprise established in 2015. With a strong reputation in automotive manufacturing and a workforce of over 50 skilled employees, the company upholds high standards of quality, innovation, and reliability.

Building on this foundation, our travel division aims to extend the same commitment to excellence by offering well-planned, personalized, and trustworthy travel experiences that showcase the beauty and culture of Sri Lanka.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to inspire travelers to discover the beauty of Sri Lanka by delivering personalized, reliable, and fully comprehensive travel solutions.

Vision Statement

To become Sri Lanka’s most trusted and preferred travel partner, creating memorable travel experiences that highlight the island’s natural beauty, culture, and hospitality.

Core Values

  • Customer-Centric Service – Your satisfaction is our priority.
  • Authenticity – Genuine Sri Lankan experiences.
  • Integrity – Honesty and transparency.
  • Reliability – Dependable, professional service.
  • Sustainability – Responsible and eco-friendly tourism.
  • Innovation – Modern approaches to enhance travel experiences.
