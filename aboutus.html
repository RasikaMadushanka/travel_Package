About Us

Discover who we are and what drives our passion for Sri Lankan travel experiences.

Our Story

Our travel agency operates under the umbrella of G.C.S. Holdings (Pvt) Ltd, a diversified Sri Lankan enterprise established in 2015. G.C.S. Holdings has earned a solid reputation in the automotive manufacturing industry, specializing in assembling airbag sensors for vehicles. With over 50 skilled professionals, the company continues to uphold high standards of quality, innovation, and reliability.

Building on this strong foundation, our travel division was founded to bring the same dedication to quality and customer satisfaction into the world of tourism — showcasing the beauty, culture, and hospitality of Sri Lanka through personalized, trustworthy travel experiences.

Our Mission

To inspire and assist travelers in exploring the diverse wonders of Sri Lanka by delivering personalized, reliable, and comprehensive travel solutions. We connect clients with the best destinations, travel partners, vehicles, and accommodations — ensuring every journey is seamless and memorable.

Our Vision

To be Sri Lanka’s most trusted and preferred travel partner, renowned for creating meaningful travel experiences that celebrate the island’s natural beauty, culture, and hospitality.

Our Core Values

Customer-Centric Service

We place our travelers at the heart of everything we do, ensuring their comfort, safety, and satisfaction.

Authenticity

We promote genuine Sri Lankan experiences that reflect the island’s culture, traditions, and natural beauty.

Reliability

We deliver dependable travel solutions through careful planning, trusted partners, and professional service.

Sustainability

Innovation
