💎 Goko Tours Gem Brokering

At Goko Tours, we specialise in offering a trusted and transparent gem brokering experience connecting travellers with Sri Lanka’s most exquisite, ethically sourced gemstones.

Working directly with licensed traders and responsible mine owners, we ensure every gemstone is genuine, ethically mined, and offered at fair market value removing unnecessary intermediaries.

Our experts guide you through sourcing, certification, pricing clarity, and selection helping you discover Sri Lanka’s world famous sapphire and gemstone heritage with confidence.
